Rideshare Partner Feed

📌 Concept demo, not a live Uber integration. Uber (and other consumer rideshare apps) don't publish a public API that lets a third party inject content into their rider-facing app. What KAIRO can do — and what this page simulates — is expose upcoming/high-demand events to a rideshare partner's own driver-supply/positioning system via an API, so their dispatch logic (not their consumer UI) knows where demand is about to spike. Clicking "Book with Uber" elsewhere in VYAVASTHA does use a real Uber deep link.
